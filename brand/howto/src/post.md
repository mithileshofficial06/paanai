Title: How to play Paanai: shape, paint and fire a pot with the whole subreddit

Images: slide-0.png to slide-5.png (in that order), then this body text.

Welcome to Paanai! Every day there is a new commission: one real vessel from pottery history. Here is how a day at the wheel works.

**Step 1: Shape it**
Open today's post and tap the button to start. Tap Open, then drag up along the wall to pull the clay taller, or drag sideways to push it in and out. Match the glowing outline. The Done button lights up when your pot passes.

**Step 2: Paint it**
Add bands, patterns, a body colour or a painted rim. Your paint stays under the glaze, so the fire will not change it.

**Step 3: Fire it**
Put it in the kiln. Every pot in the community fires together at 00:00 UTC. Your very first pot gets a 10-second test firing, so you see the whole loop on day one.

**Step 4: Open it**
Come back the next day and open the kiln. The fire picks the glaze. Find a glaze nobody has found yet and your name goes in the Codex.

**Then show it off**
Put your pot on show so others can rate and like it, share it to the day's thread, practise on 100 levels, or post one of your pots as a challenge for others to beat.

**Quick tips**
Clay dries as you work. Tap Water if it stops moving.
Walls that are too thin crack in the fire.
Your first throw each day is ranked. After that it is practice.
Have fun, and be kind about other people's pots.
