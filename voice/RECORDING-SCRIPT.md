# Tutorial voice: recording script

Recorded by Mithilesh on 2026-11-15. The tutorial now has 4 steps and speaks lines 01-12; lines 13-18 (the old practice step) were recorded but are no longer used.

18 short lines. Record each one as its own file and name it by its number (01, 02 ... 18).
Read the words exactly as written: the game matches each clip to its line.

## How to record

- Use your phone's voice recorder app. Hold the phone about a hand's width (15-20 cm) from your mouth, slightly to the side so your breath doesn't hit it.
- A quiet room with soft things around (curtains, a bed, cupboards) sounds best. Turn off fans and the AC for a few minutes.
- Speak like you are showing a friend how to play: relaxed, friendly, a little slower than normal, smiling slightly.
- Leave about one second of silence before and after each line. I trim it.
- Made a mistake? Just record that line again and keep the better file.
- Put all 18 files in `D:\Projects\2-In-Progress\paanai\voice\raw\` (by USB, Google Drive, or WhatsApp Web download) and tell me. I clean, trim and level them.

## The lines

| File | Say this |
|:---:|---|
| 01 | How it works. Each level asks for one vessel. A hundred levels, each a little harder than the last. |
| 02 | Shape the spinning clay to match the glowing outline. Hold to centre it, then tap Open. |
| 03 | This is the pass mark. Score above it to clear the level. |
| 04 | Hands, Push and Thin. Hands: drag up along the wall to pull the pot taller. |
| 05 | Push: drag sideways to swell the belly out or squeeze a neck in. |
| 06 | Thin: thins the wall. Too thin and the pot wobbles, then collapses. |
| 07 | Tap Water when the clay dries out. Undo takes back your last stroke. |
| 08 | Scoring and the leaderboard. Matching the shape is most of your score. Even walls, the rules, and using few strokes add the rest. |
| 09 | Tap Done to score: up to three stars a level. The daily commission also ranks you on today's leaderboard. |
| 10 | After you tap Done. Clear a level and the next one unlocks on the Levels map. Replay any level for more stars. |
| 11 | Every day there is also a daily commission. Paint it and fire it in the kiln overnight. |
| 12 | Tap here to watch this again. Now it is your turn. |
| 13 | Press and hold on the clay until it stops wobbling. |
| 14 | Tap Open to press a hollow into the middle. |
| 15 | With Hands, drag up along the wall, two or three times, to pull it taller. |
| 16 | Tap Push, then drag the middle of the wall outward. |
| 17 | Happy with it? Tap Done. |
| 18 | Well thrown! The tutorial is complete. Enter level one when you are ready. |

Tip for lines 01, 04, 08 and 10: the first words are the section name ("How it works.", "Hands, Push and Thin.", ...). Pause briefly after them, like a heading.
